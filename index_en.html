AURUMEN – Studio

AURUMEN is an artistic studio founded by Julius Wachter and Anna Szekely, dedicated to the development and realization of three-dimensional wall objects of the highest calibre. The works emerge from a refined synthesis of material, structure and visual effects. They are conceived not as objects in the classical sense, but as living, spatial systems in which perception, depth and movement continually shift for the viewer.

The creations of AURUMEN are based on bespoke constructions where material, form and surface constitute an inseparable unity. Depending on the conceptual direction, the presentation ranges from frameless, purist elegance to custom-fabricated enclosures. Where frames are employed, they are created in close collaboration with a specialized partner, precisely calibrated to the respective object, and contribute significantly to the overall presence within the space.

Underpinning this artistic vision is many years of rigorous research and development. For several years, Julius Wachter and Anna Szekely have worked intensively with resin as an artistic medium. In an autonomous process, numerous methods were tested, refined and reimagined. This has yielded specific techniques and technological solutions that form the foundation of their contemporary oeuvre.

Julius Wachter brings an extraordinary combination of artisanal and technical experience to the studio. His multifaceted background encompasses copper-working, master carpentry, and scenic design for theatre. He has developed and manufactured crystal chandeliers and lighting fixtures, and has worked as a conservator. Furthermore, as a graduate engineer in plant construction, he directed projects in international shipbuilding, notably serving as plant manager in the luxury ocean liner sector. This profound expertise shapes the precision, structural clarity and deep technical quality that are palpable in every detail of the artworks.

Every creation by AURUMEN is entirely unique. The themes deliberately vary and do not adhere to a rigid series, for the decisive factor is not the motif, but the pure relationship between material, space and the interaction with the environment. The works are executed in various formats and integrate harmoniously into sophisticated architectural contexts. In addition, individual pieces can be bespoke and developed in close alignment with the specific space. AURUMEN thus represents an entirely independent position at the intersection of art, material research and technical innovation.

This artistic evolution unfolds across three distinct conceptual lines:

RESONANCE (Echo Matter)

This collection deliberately dispenses with any frame, captivating the viewer through pure, minimalist elegance. The foundation is a deep black panel, refined through a sophisticated process. Upon this flawless, highly reflective substrate of noble resin, the three-dimensional objects are staged. Care Instructions for Your Artwork

This artwork has been meticulously handcrafted from high-gloss resin and/or thermally formed acrylic glass. To preserve the flawless quality of these refined materials and maintain the brilliance of the colours for generations to come, please observe the following recommendations:

Cleaning: For routine care, use only an exceptionally soft, clean microfibre cloth (dry or lightly dampened with distilled water).

Avoid: Never use chemical cleaning agents, glass cleaners, alcohol-based products, or abrasive sponges. These may irreparably scratch or dull the high-gloss surfaces.

Placement: The artwork is designed exclusively for indoor display. Avoid positioning it directly above strong heat sources (e.g. radiators) or in areas with excessively high humidity.

Light Exposure: Although we use only UV-stabilised premium-grade components, we recommend avoiding prolonged exposure to intense direct sunlight in order to preserve the vibrancy and luminosity of the pigments for decades to come.

FAQ / Frequently Asked Questions

How is the artwork powered and recharged? The artwork features an integrated, concealed high-end rechargeable battery system. It can be easily recharged using a standard USB Type-C charging cable (a premium design cable is included with the artwork).

How long does the artwork remain illuminated on a single charge? Thanks to state-of-the-art, highly efficient LED technology and high-capacity rechargeable batteries, the artwork provides approximately 4 to 8 hours of continuous illumination, depending on the selected brightness level and lighting mode.

How are the different lighting effects controlled? Depending on the model and configuration of the artwork, a specially matched remote control is included. For artworks featuring a single lighting layer (e.g. pure RGB COB lighting or pure UV illumination), brightness and colour settings can be controlled directly via infrared (IR) or radio-frequency (RF) remote control. For our more advanced, multi-layer future-oriented works, an exclusive multi-zone wireless system enables completely independent control and dimming of the various lighting layers.

Are the LEDs replaceable? We exclusively use industrial-grade LEDs with a service life exceeding 50,000 hours. With daily use of four hours, this corresponds to a lifespan of more than 34 years. Should a technical issue nevertheless occur, the electronics compartment has been designed to remain accessible for authorised service professionals.
